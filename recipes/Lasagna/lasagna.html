Lasagna Recipe

Home



Description

Lasagna is a hearty Italian dish made by layering pasta sheets with rich tomato sauce, savory meat or vegetables, creamy béchamel sauce, and melted cheese. The layers are baked together, creating a comforting and flavorful meal that’s loved around the world.


Ingredients:
  • Meat
  • Oninon and garlic
  • Tomato products
  • Sugar
  • Spices and seasoning
  • Lasagna noodles
  • Cheeses
  • Eggs

Steps:
  1. Make the meat sauce
  2. Cook the noodles
  3. Make the ricotta mixture
  4. Layer the lasagna according to the recipe instructions
  5. Cover with foil and bake
  6. Let the lasagna rest before serving
